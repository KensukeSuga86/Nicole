## Hipparcos Planetarium Data Creator / Stellarium-derived constellation-line data

Nicole Astronomy Database v0.3.0 formalizes Nicole's constellation-line topology after manual editing in Nicole the Astrorium v0.7.6. The baseline line dataset and line-star catalog used by Nicole originate from:

- Hipparcos Planetarium Data Creator: https://github.com/creativival/hipparcos_planetarium_data_creator
- `hip_constellation_line.csv`
- `hip_constellation_line_star.csv`
- Upstream states that the constellation data is derived from Stellarium data and is provided under GNU GPL v2.0.

The GPL v2.0 license text is included at:
`LICENSES/Hipparcos-Planetarium-Data-Creator-GPL-2.0.txt`
